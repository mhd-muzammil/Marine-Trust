Volunteer Data Deletion Request

This page applies to the Conserve Marine Biodiversity mobile application.

Our application does not require account creation or login. However, users may voluntarily submit personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Volunteer activity details

How to Request Data Deletion

If you wish to request deletion of your submitted volunteer information, please send an email from your registered email address to:

Email: worldmarinebiodiversity@gmail.com

Subject: Volunteer Data Deletion Request

Please include the following details:

  • Your full name
  • Email address used in the app
  • Phone number (if provided)

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Volunteer form details
  • Personal contact information
  • Associated activity submissions

Deletion Timeline

All verified deletion requests will be permanently processed within 7 working days.

Data deletion requests can be submitted even if the mobile application has been uninstalled.
